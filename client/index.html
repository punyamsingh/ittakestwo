How to play

Two dolls, one thread

  • WASDMove
  • SpaceJump
  • ShiftHold to brace
  • EscPause

  • Rose’s red thread ties May and Cody together. Wander too far and it drags your partner along.

  • Brace to plant your feet. Nothing moves you — not drafts, not your partner hanging below.

  • Climb by pressing Jump while you dangle below a braced partner.
